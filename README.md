# Decoding-Customer-DNA-Retail-Behavior-Analytics
Analyzing customer purchase behavior to uncover insights that drive smarter marketing, stronger loyalty, and higher revenue.

---

## 📖 Overview

This project explores how data-driven insights can help a retail company understand its customers better and optimize key business decisions.  
Using **Python**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**, I analyzed 3.9K+ customer transactions to uncover purchase patterns, discount effectiveness, and loyalty segments — transforming raw data into actionable business intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand factors influencing customer purchasing decisions.
- Segment customers by demographics, loyalty, and spend patterns.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts, shipping types, and subscriptions on revenue.
- Create an interactive dashboard for real-time business insights.

---

| Tool / Language | Purpose |
|------------------|----------|

## ⚙️ Workflow

### 1️⃣ Data Preparation (Python)
- Cleaned 3.9K+ records and handled missing values using median imputation.  
- Created **age group** and **purchase frequency** features for deeper insights.  
- Exported transformed data to PostgreSQL for SQL-based analysis.

### 2️⃣ Data Analysis (SQL)
Conducted business-focused queries to reveal:
- 💸 **Revenue trends** by gender, subscription status, and shipping type.  
- 🏷️ **Discount effectiveness** — 839 customers used discounts yet spent above average.  
- ⭐ **Top 5 high-rated products** driving repeat purchases.  
- 👥 **Customer segmentation** — New, Returning, and Loyal customers.  
- 📊 **Revenue contribution by age group** showing decline with age.

### 3️⃣ Visualization (Power BI)
- Designed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** summarizing:
  - Revenue by demographics and loyalty
  - Purchase frequency trends
  - Discount vs. non-discount behavior
  - Product performance and ratings

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- 🧾 **Non-subscribers spent 15% more** than subscribers.  
- 💰 **Discount users generated 25% of total revenue**.  
- 🛍️ Identified **top 5 products** with the highest customer satisfaction and retention.  
- 📉 Revenue declines with age, suggesting targeted campaigns for younger segments.

---

## 🧠 Business Impact

These insights enable:
- Targeted promotions for high-value segments  
- Optimized discount strategies without eroding profit margins  
- Personalized engagement plans to convert one-time buyers into loyal customers
